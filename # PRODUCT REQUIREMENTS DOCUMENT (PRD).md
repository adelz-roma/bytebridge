# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

# 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

# 1.1 Product Description

The Student Storage and Logistics Platform is a web-based solution designed to help students conveniently store and retrieve their personal belongings during school breaks, holidays, and other periods when they are away from campus.

The platform connects students with storage facilities and logistics providers, allowing them to book storage space and choose how their belongings are transported to and from the storage facility.

Students can either request pickup from their halls of residence or deliver their belongings to the storage facility themselves. Similarly, they can request delivery when retrieving their belongings or collect them directly from the facility.


# 1.2 Product Vision

To provide students with a convenient, accessible, secure, and reliable way to store and manage their personal belongings while away from school.

# 1.3 Product Objectives

- Provide students with convenient access to storage facilities.
- Reduce the stress associated with transporting personal belongings during school breaks.
- Connect students with reliable logistics providers.
- Allow students to book storage spaces according to their needs.
- Provide flexible pickup, delivery, and self-collection options.
- Improve the overall storage and retrieval experience.

# 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Students who travel from different locations to attend Babcock, the survey reveals other states like Rivers State; port harcourt, Anambra State, Lagos State and Abuja. They often face difficulties managing their personal belongings when leaving campus for holidays or extended breaks.

Some students have to transport everything back home, while others struggle to find secure and convenient places to keep their belongings.

Common challenges include:

- Difficulty transporting heavy or multiple bags.
- High transportation costs.
- Lack of accessible and reliable storage facilities.
- Concerns about the security of personal belongings.
- Difficulty arranging luggage pickup and delivery.
- Inconvenience of travelling to retrieve stored belongings.

There is a need for a centralized platform that simplifies the process of booking storage space and arranging luggage transportation.

## 3. PROPOSED SOLUTION

The proposed platform will provide a centralized system where students can:

1. Create an account.
2. Browse available storage options.
3. Select and book suitable storage space.
4. Choose between hostel pickup and self-delivery.
5. Submit their luggage details.
6. Arrange delivery or self-collection when retrieving their belongings.
7. Monitor their storage bookings and requests.

The platform will also provide logistics providers and administrators with tools to manage transportation requests, storage bookings, and customer records.

# 5. PRODUCT SCOPE

Product Features & User Stories

# 5.1 Core Product Features

This platform will provide the following features to help students store and retrieve their personal belongings conveniently:
User Registration: Students, storage providers and logistics personnel can create accounts and log in.
Storage Listings: Storage providers can display their available spaces, prices, locations and descriptions.
Storage Booking: Students can search for book storage spaces based on their needs and duration.
Pickup and Delivery: Students can either transport their belongings themselves or request assistance from logistics personnel.
Booking Management: Users can view and manage their bookings and requests.
Payment and Notifications: Students can make payments and receive updates on bookings, pickup and delivery.

# 5.2 MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
  The first version of the website will focus on the essential features needed to make the platform functional:

User registration and login.
Display of available storage spaces.
Storage booking.
Pickup and delivery requests.
Booking management.
Payment and booking confirmation.

# 5.3 Future Features
Additional features that may be introduced in future versions include:

Real-time tracking of belongings.
Ratings and reviews for storage and logistics providers.
In-platform chat between users.
Photo inventory of stored belongings.
Automated reminders for collection dates and storage expiry.
Storage extension requests.

# 5.4 User Stories

Student 1: As a student, I want to find and book available storage spaces so that I can safely keep my belongings when I am away.
Student 2: As a student, I want to choose between self-delivery and logistics pickup so that I can use whichever option is more convenient.
Student 3: As a student, I want to request delivery of my belongings so that I can retrieve them when needed.
Storage Provider: As a storage provider, I want to list and manage my storage spaces so that students can book them.
Logistics Provider: As a logistics provider, I want to view and accept pickup delivery requests so that I can assist students in transporting their belongings.


# 5.5 User Journey and Workflows

Student Workflow:
Student: Registers → Browses storage spaces → Selects a space → Chooses storage duration and delivery option → Makes payment → Drops off or arranges pickup → Retrieves belongings.

Storage Provider: Registers → Lists storage space → Receives booking → Confirms booking → Stores belongings → Makes belongings available for collection.

Logistics Provider: Registers → Views requests → Accepts request → Picks up or delivers belongings → Updates request status.

# 5.6 Business Rules
Users must register before accessing booking services.
Storage spaces must be available before they can be booked.
Every booking must have a storage duration.
Logistics services are optional.
Bookings must be confirmed after successful payment.
Logistics requests must be accepted before a provider is assigned.

# 5.7 Acceptance Criteria
Users can register and log in.
Students can view and book available storage spaces.
Unavailable spaces cannot be booked.
Students can choose self-delivery or request pickup.
Payments and bookings can be confirmed.
Storage providers can manage listings and bookings.
Logistics providers can accept request and update their status.

# USER FLOW

1. User Registration and Authentication
Stackup will provide separate registration processes for students and logistics agencies. Firebase Authentication will be used to manage account creation and login through email addresses.
a.	Student Registration:
Students will be required to provide:
•	Full name
•	School/University
•	Department
•	Hostel or residential address
•	Email address
•	Phone number
•	Password

b.	Logistics Agency Registration:
Agencies will provide:
•	Agency/business name
•	Representative's full name
•	Business address
•	Email address
•	Phone number
•	Areas of operation
•	Password

2. User Login and Account Management
Registered students and logistics agencies will be able to log into their accounts using their registered email addresses and passwords.
Users will also be able to:
•	Log out of their accounts.
•	View their profile information.
•	Update their personal or business information.
•	Reset forgotten passwords.

3. Logistics Agency Listings
Students will be able to view registered logistics agencies available on the platform based on their budget and the location they are going.
Each agency listing will display:
•	Agency name.
•	Description of services.
•	Contact information.
•	Location.
•	Areas covered.
•	Available services.
•	Pricing information, where provided.
This will help students compare available agencies before making a request.

4. Service Request
Students will be able to submit requests for logistics services through the website.
The request form will require:
•	Description of belongings.
•	Type of service required.
•	Pickup location.
•	Picture of luggages
•	Destination or storage location.
•	Preferred date.
•	Additional instructions.
Students will be able to submit their requests to their preferred logistics agencies.

5. Pickup and Drop-off Options
Stackup will provide two service options:
A. Pickup Service
Students who cannot transport their belongings themselves can request pickup from their hostel or preferred location. An additional pickup fee may apply.
B. Self Drop-off
Students who prefer to take their belongings to the logistics agency can select the drop-off option. The agency will provide its location so the student can deliver the belongings.

6. Request Management and Status Tracking
Students will be able to view the status of their service requests.
The request statuses will include:
•	Pending: The request has been submitted.
•	Accepted: The logistics agency has accepted the request.
•	In Progress: The service is currently being carried out.
•	Completed: The service has been completed.
•	Rejected: The agency cannot fulfil the request.
Logistics agencies will also have access to incoming requests and will be able to accept or reject them.

7. Payment Information
Students will be able to view the estimated cost of their requested services before confirming their requests.
The cost may depend on:
•	Type of service.
•	Quantity or size of belongings.
•	Distance covered.
•	Additional pickup charges.
The platform will display the agreed cost and payment status. Actual online payment processing can be considered for a later version if it is not essential to the first release.

8. Receipt Generation
After a transaction has been confirmed, Stackup will generate a digital receipt containing:
•	Student's name.
•	Logistics agency name.
•	Description of belongings.
•	Service requested.
•	Agreed amount.
•	Transaction date.
•	Unique transaction reference.
Students will be able to view and download their receipts.

9. Notifications
Students and logistics agencies will receive notifications about important activities, such as:
•	New service requests.
•	Request acceptance or rejection.
•	Changes in request status.
•	Service completion.

10. Agency Verification
To promote trust and reliability, logistics agencies will be required to submit relevant business information for verification.
An administrator will review submitted information before approving an agency to provide services through Stackup.

# 8 BUSINESS & LOGISTICS OPERATIONS 
8.1 Storage Management
StackUp will connect students with existing storage agencies rather than operate its own storage facilities.
•	Agencies must register and undergo verification before joining the platform.
•	Verified agencies will manage their own profiles and provide information about their storage facilities and arrangements.
•	Agencies will determine their available storage capacity and update it when necessary.
•	The platform will track capacity based on approved bookings and recorded storage activity.
•	Students will not be able to submit booking requests to agencies that have reached their declared capacity.
•	Agencies will be responsible for belongings accepted into their custody.

8.2 Logistics Partnerships
StackUp will support agencies that provide storage and transportation services, either independently or through partnerships with established logistics agencies.
•	Agencies must disclose their transportation arrangements.
•	Logistics arrangements must undergo verification before being offered to students.
•	Storage and logistics agencies will coordinate the movement of students' belongings.
•	Delivery will be the designated method of returning belongings to students.
•	Applicable transportation and delivery charges must be communicated before booking confirmation.

8.3 Pricing Management
Agencies will determine their own storage rates and payment deadlines.
Storage charges will be calculated based on:
•	Number of items submitted.
•	Size of each item.
The platform will calculate estimated charges using the selected agency's pricing structure.
Agencies may also determine additional transportation and delivery charges.
Group bookings will be eligible for agency-defined discounts. Each student will retain individual payment responsibility, while the agency reviews the combined group request. If a member fails to meet the payment deadline, the group discount will be recalculated.

8.4 Booking and Payment Operations
Students may make individual or group booking requests.
•	Individual requests will be reviewed and approved or declined by the selected agency.
•	Group requests will be submitted as one combined request and reviewed as a whole.
•	Approved students will receive payment instructions and the agency's payment deadline.
•	Students will submit payment evidence through the platform.
•	The agency must verify receipt of payment before the booking is confirmed.
•	Agencies will record belongings received and update their storage records accordingly.

8.5 Retrieval and Agency Responsibility
Agencies will coordinate the return of stored belongings through delivery to the student's chosen address.
•	Agencies will communicate delivery arrangements and applicable charges.
•	Successful delivery must be recorded before a booking is marked completed.
•	Agencies will be responsible for investigating issues involving damaged, lost or missing belongings while in their custody.
•	The platform will provide a channel for students to submit complaints and monitor their resolution.

8.6 Administrative Oversight
Platform administrators will oversee agency verification, account management and compliance with platform requirements. They will also monitor reported complaints and take appropriate action where agencies violate platform policies.

8.7 Outstanding Operational Decisions
The following details require further clarification before implementation:
•	Payment processing and how agencies receive payments.
•	How the platform generates revenue.
•	The method agencies will use to measure storage capacity.
•	Specific requirements for verifying logistics arrangements.
•	Procedures for handling late retrievals and delivery disputes.
